# Frameworks Reference

These are the thinking frameworks Hugh uses. Apply them where relevant without referencing them explicitly to the person being written to or advised.

---

## DISC

A behavioural profiling framework. Four profiles:

**D (Dominance):** Results-focused, direct, decisive, impatient with detail. Wants the bottom line fast. Responds to clarity, brevity, and confidence. Less preamble, clearer call to action.

**I (Influence):** People-focused, enthusiastic, collaborative, optimistic. Motivated by relationships and recognition. Warmer tone, acknowledge the relationship, not just the task.

**S (Steadiness):** Reliable, patient, team-oriented, resistant to sudden change. Needs reassurance and context before acting. Warmer, more gradual tone. Avoid abruptness.

**C (Conscientiousness):** Analytical, detail-oriented, quality-focused, risk-aware. Needs data, reasoning, and evidence. More context and structured logic. Avoid vague or unsupported claims.

**Application rule:** Use DISC to shape tone, pacing, and approach when writing to someone. Never reference it directly to them. When writing about someone to a third party, DISC references are appropriate.

## Working Genius

A productivity and team framework by Patrick Lencioni. Six types of work genius:

**Wonder:** Asking the big questions, identifying what's possible, pondering

**Invention:** Creating new ideas and solutions from scratch

**Discernment:** Instinctively evaluating ideas and sensing what will and won't work

**Galvanising:** Rallying people to take action and move things forward

**Enablement:** Supporting and assisting others to achieve their goals

**Tenacity:** Pushing through to completion and following through on commitments

**Application rule:** Use to understand what energises or drains people on a team. Useful in coaching, hiring, and team structure conversations.

## EOS (Entrepreneurial Operating System)

A business operating framework. Key language:

- **Rocks:** 90-day priorities — the most important things to get done this quarter
- **Level 10 Meeting:** Weekly team meeting with a fixed agenda and health score
- **Traction:** Consistent execution over time
- **Visionary / Integrator:** The two key leadership roles — the ideas person and the operator
- **Right Person, Right Seat:** Someone who fits the culture and is in a role that matches their strengths
- **Issues List:** Problems that need solving — raised, discussed, solved (IDS)
- **Scorecard:** Weekly measurables that show whether the business is on track

**Application rule:** Use EOS language naturally when it fits the context. Hugh operates in an EOS-adjacent way — it doesn't need to be named explicitly.

---

## PHD — Pig-Headed Discipline

Hugh's personal operating principle. Doing what needs to be done, consistently, without excuses, even when it's hard or unglamorous. Applied to habits, standards, and follow-through across all work.
