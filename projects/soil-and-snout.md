# Project: Soil & Snout

## What It Is

A premium Australian pet-safe home care brand. Products that work and are safe for the animals that live in your home. Not a compromise product — genuinely effective, genuinely safe.

## Current Status

Pre-launch. Supplier sourcing in progress. RFQ-001 sent to 26 manufacturers for frosted/smoked glass trigger spray bottles. Supplier tracker built and active.

## The Goal

Launch a premium home care brand in the Australian market with pet safety as the non-negotiable foundation.

## Target Customer or Audience

Australian homeowners who have pets and care about what they're cleaning with. Premium buyer — not price-sensitive, values quality and safety.

## Key Constraints

Side business alongside MEX Engineering Group. Time and bandwidth are real constraints. Premium positioning must be maintained — do not drift toward mass-market thinking.

## What Not to Assume

- Do not suggest discount or volume-led strategies
- Pet safety is not a feature or a differentiator — it is the brand's entire reason for existing
- Australia first — do not default to US market assumptions

## Active Priorities

1. Finalise supplier selection for trigger spray bottles
2. Define initial product range
3. Build brand identity and packaging direction
