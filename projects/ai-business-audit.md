# Project: AI Business Audit

## What It Is

A paid inbound voice-led diagnostic product. Business owners call in after seeing an ad, a voice agent asks diagnostic questions, answers are transcribed, and a personalised AI readiness report is generated. Hugh calls the customer back to discuss the report and offer implementation services.

## Current Status

Phases 1-3 complete in software. Phase 3 (voice flow via Retell) blocked pending SIPCity inbound number setup and public webhook deployment. 1300 number being pursued.

## The Goal

A self-qualifying lead generation engine. The $299 price filters for serious buyers before Hugh invests time in a callback. Report delivery builds trust and pre-sells capability. The callback closes the next step.

## Target Customer or Audience

Electrical trades businesses in Australia — first niche. Owners who are curious about AI but don't know where to start or what's worth their time.

## Offer Ladder

1. AI Readiness Assessment — $299
2. Implementation Sprint — TBD
3. Monthly Retainer (automations, agents, advisory) — TBD

## Key Constraints

- Hugh personally does the callback — capacity is a real constraint as volume grows
- Report must be specific and actionable, not boilerplate — generic advice kills credibility
- Voice agent question set drives report quality — bad questions, generic report

## What Not to Assume

- This is not a free lead magnet
- Do not suggest lowering the price to increase volume
- The report is the product — the callback is the sales conversation

## Active Priorities

1. Finalise inbound number setup (SIPCity / 1300 AI HELP)
2. Deploy public webhook for Retell integration
3. Design the 8-12 diagnostic questions for electrical trades

## Tech Stack

Retell AI (voice agent), SIPCity (inbound number), Claude API (report generation), n8n (workflow), custom web dashboard
