# Project: MEX Engineering Group

## What It Is

Electrical and mechanical engineering services for industrial and commercial clients in Sydney, Australia. Trading as MEX Engineering Group, legal entity King Electrical Services Pty Ltd. Core services include maintenance, compliance, machine safety, and trade services.

## Current Status

Established and revenue-generating. Strategic repositioning underway. No deliberate business development engine in place. Revenue heavily concentrated in legacy shift-cover clients.

## The Goal

Build a deliberate BD engine. Shift revenue mix toward higher-margin machine safety and compliance work, and recurring maintenance SLA contracts. Machine safety is the front door that pulls trade remediation and SLA revenue behind it.

## Target Customer or Audience

Industrial and commercial clients requiring electrical and mechanical maintenance, compliance, and machine safety services. Sydney-based initially.

## Key Differentiator

One of four CMSE (Certified Machinery Safety Expert) TÜV-Nord certified people in the business. Rare credential in the Australian market.

## Key Constraints

Founder/owner philosophically favours relationship-building over direct sales. Revenue already exists — the challenge is mix and margin, not starting from zero. The team executes in the field — operational complexity is real.

## What Not to Assume

- This is not a startup
- Do not suggest generic sales tactics that ignore relationship-first culture
- Machine safety is a compliance requirement, not just a service offering — the regulatory angle matters

## Active Priorities

1. Build the machine safety BD pipeline
2. Develop costed SLA configurator for recurring revenue conversations
3. Position machine safety credential in market-facing materials

## Company Language

Pig-Headed Discipline, People First, Home Safe Always, Right Seat on the Bus, Solutions Focused, Excellence over Perfection
